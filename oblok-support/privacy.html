Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026 · Applies from app version 1.3.6 (build 36) · Developer: DRK STUDIO · Contact: destek@drksistem.com

This Privacy Policy explains how DRK STUDIO ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Oblok (the "Game", package id ai.drksistem.oblok), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

In short: Oblok has no player accounts and no game backend of its own — your progress is stored on your device. Advertising is served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation and its partner ad networks; measurement uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Ad personalisation is optional: declining does not remove ads, it only makes them non-personalised, and you can change the choice at any time under Settings → Privacy. Optional purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple.

What changed in this version

Until August 2026 the Game served ads through Google AdMob and asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Both have been removed. The google_mobile_ads component is no longer part of the Game. Advertising now runs on Unity LevelPlay mediation, and the personalisation choice is collected by an in-game dialog described in section 1(e).

1. Information We Handle

(a) Information you provide directly. The Game does not require sign-up, login, name, email address, or a player account. If you contact support by email, we receive the address and information included in that message and use it to answer your request.

(b) Data stored locally on your device. The Game stores level progress, stars, daily-reward and streak state, booster inventory, block collection, settings, your ad personalisation choice, and advertising frequency counters in on-device storage. This local save data is not uploaded as a save file and is removed if you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Some limited gameplay values are also included in analytics events as explained in section 1(f).

(c) Advertising through Unity LevelPlay. The Game uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) as its ad mediation platform. Two ad formats are used: rewarded ads, which are always voluntary and only start when you choose them, and interstitial ads, which appear only after a level you have won, never during play, never in levels 1–10 of the Adventure Path, and at most 8 times per calendar day. Rewarded ads are limited to 25 views per calendar day as an anti-abuse safeguard.

Depending on your region, your personalisation choice, your device settings, and the networks that respond to a given ad request, LevelPlay and the partner networks listed in section 5 may process: an advertising identifier (on Android the Google Advertising ID or App Set ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking), IP address and a coarse location derived from it, device and app information such as model, operating system version, app version, language and country, ad requests, impressions, interactions, viewability and diagnostic signals, and impression-level revenue data.

(d) Our own in-app promotion (no ad network involved). When you are online but no network ad is available, the Game plays a video bundled inside the app that promotes our own app Altın Asistanım. This fallback makes no ad request, contacts no ad network, uses no advertising identifier and is never personalised. For a rewarded offer, the reward is granted once the clip has been watched to at least 90%.

(e) Ad personalisation choice. The Game no longer uses Google's User Messaging Platform. Instead, immediately before the first real network ad, the Game shows its own dialog asking whether ads may be personalised. Your answer is stored on the device and passed to LevelPlay as a per-network GDPR consent signal (IronSource, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Amazon Publisher Services, Verve and Yandex), as the CCPA "do not sell my personal information" flag, and together with a COPPA declaration that the Game is not directed to children.

Declining does not switch ads off — it only switches personalisation off. Until you answer, ads are treated as non-personalised. You can change the choice at any time from Settings → Privacy inside the Game; the entry is always available, and changing it discards any ad that was already loaded under the previous choice. On iOS, the system App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown only if you accepted personalisation; if you declined, the prompt is never shown and ads continue without tracking.

(f) Firebase Analytics. The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand retention, level balance, and advertising performance. Analytics data may include an app-instance identifier, device and app information, language, session and screen events, level and mode, attempt number, objective, move and time limits, remaining moves or time, the extra-move compensation given after a loss, board-fill and progress percentages, win, loss and abandonment reasons, booster use, daily-reward day and streak, wheel spins, the ad funnel (offer seen → requested → shown → watched → rewarded, with ad placement and format), purchase-funnel events with product, local price and currency, and impression-level ad revenue reported by LevelPlay in micro-units of currency. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, payment-card details, contacts, photos, or message content to Firebase. On Firebase Test Lab devices — used for the automated pre-launch report — analytics and the ad SDK are switched off automatically.

(g) Firebase Crashlytics. If the Game crashes or hits a fatal error, a crash report is sent so we can fix it. A report contains the stack trace, device model, operating system and app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. It does not contain your name, email address, or game content.

(h) Purchases. Optional in-app purchases (three consumable booster packs) are processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive transaction status and product information needed to deliver the purchase; we do not receive or store your payment-card details. Because all three products are consumable and the Game has no player account, spent balances cannot be restored across devices.

The Game does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Advertising and analytics providers may infer an approximate location from an IP address as part of their services.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the Game, save local progress, and provide requested rewards or purchases.
  • To record and respect your ad personalisation choice on every ad request.
  • To serve, pace, cap, and measure rewarded and interstitial ads.
  • To protect advertisers and ourselves against invalid traffic — for example by backing off after failed ad requests, throttling repeated error reports, not requesting ads while the app is in the background, capping daily impressions, and inserting a short delay so an ad cannot catch a tap meant for the game.
  • To measure retention, level balance, failed flows, ad performance, and revenue.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.
  • To improve levels, rewards, and advertising frequency.
  • To schedule a local daily reminder notification if you allow notifications.

3. Advertising Consent and Your Choices

The Game asks for your ad personalisation choice in its own dialog, shown right before the first real network ad rather than at start-up. You can review or change that choice at any time under Settings → Privacy.

You can also use your device controls: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete the advertising ID; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls App Tracking Transparency. Withdrawing consent changes the type of ads you see, not whether the Game works — rewarded offers and their rewards remain available without personalisation.

4. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — ads, analytics, crash reports, and purchases; also used to detect whether you are offline before an ad is offered.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — advertising identifier where available and permitted.
  • VIBRATE — optional haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — an optional daily reminder scheduled locally on the device at 19:30; no server is involved and no notification data leaves the device. You may decline or disable it.

5. Service Providers and Disclosure

We do not operate a market for personal information and we do not sell it. We disclose data to the providers below as needed for the services described in this policy. Which ad networks take part in a given ad request varies by region, by your personalisation choice, and by the LevelPlay configuration in force at the time. Some privacy laws describe personalised advertising as "sharing" or "selling"; the choice described in section 3 is the control available to you.

6. Retention and Security

Local save data remains on your device until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Analytics, crash, advertising, consent, and transaction information may be retained by the providers listed in section 5 according to their own settings and retention policies. We limit event fields to what is useful for operating and improving the Game, but no electronic processing method is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Oblok is a casual puzzle game intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. The Game declares itself as not directed to children to the mediation platform, so child-directed treatment is not requested on its behalf. We do not knowingly ask children for personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the issue.

8. International Processing

The providers listed in section 5 may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy documentation describes the safeguards they apply to international data transfers.

9. Your Requests

You can erase local game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, disable notifications in device settings, change ad personalisation under Settings → Privacy, and reset or delete your advertising identifier with your device controls. For questions or privacy requests, contact us at destek@drksistem.com. Because the Game has no player account, we cannot link a request to a specific player profile; please do not send sensitive identifiers unless support specifically requires them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date above identifies the latest revision.
