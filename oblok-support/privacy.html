Privacy Policy — Oblok

Last updated: 13 July 2026 · Developer: DRK STUDIO · Contact: destek@drksistem.com

This Privacy Policy explains how DRK STUDIO ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Oblok (the "Game", package id ai.drksistem.oblok), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

In short: Oblok has no player accounts and no game backend of its own. Game progress is stored on your device. The Game does, however, use Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, and Firebase Analytics. These services process advertising, consent, device, usage, and gameplay-event data as described below. Optional purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple.

1. Information We Handle

(a) Information you provide directly. The Game does not require sign-up, login, name, email address, or a player account. If you contact support by email, we receive the address and information included in that message and use it to answer your request.

(b) Data stored locally on your device. The Game stores level progress, scores, stars, streak and daily-reward state, booster inventory, settings, advertising frequency state, and gameplay counters in on-device storage. This local save data is not uploaded as a save file and is removed if you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Some limited gameplay values are also included in Firebase Analytics events as explained below.

(c) Data processed by Google AdMob. The Game uses AdMob for optional rewarded ads and for interstitial ads shown at eligible level-completion breaks. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, ad requests, ad impressions, interactions, diagnostics, and impression-level revenue data. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized according to the choices and legal requirements that apply to you.

(d) Consent and privacy choices. The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request updated consent information, display a consent form when required, determine whether advertising requests are allowed, and make privacy options available again when required. Google processes the consent and privacy signals needed to provide this service.

(e) Firebase Analytics. The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand stability, retention, level balance, and advertising performance. Analytics data may include an app-instance identifier, device/app information, language, session and screen events, level number and objective, wins and losses, score, moves, play duration, board-fill or progress percentages, booster use, daily-reward activity, ad request/show/reward/result events, purchase-funnel events, and impression-level ad revenue. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, payment-card details, contacts, photos, or message content to Firebase.

(f) Purchases. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive transaction status and product information needed to deliver the purchase; we do not receive or store your payment-card details.

The Game does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Google may infer an approximate location from an IP address as part of its advertising and analytics services.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the Game, save local progress, and provide requested rewards or purchases.
  • To request and respect advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • To serve, limit, measure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of rewarded and interstitial ads.
  • To measure retention, gameplay balance, failed flows, ad performance, and revenue.
  • To improve levels, rewards, advertising frequency, and user acquisition decisions.
  • To schedule a local daily reminder notification if you allow notifications.

3. Advertising Consent and Your Choices

Where required, the Game displays a Google UMP message before requesting ads. You can use the Game's Privacy options entry when it is shown to review available choices. You can also use your device's advertising and privacy controls, including controls for the advertising identifier and ad personalization. Withdrawing or refusing consent may change the types of ads available and, where an ad cannot lawfully be requested, an ad-funded reward may not be available.

4. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — ads, analytics, consent, and purchases.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — advertising identifier where available and permitted.
  • VIBRATE — optional haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional local daily reminder; you may decline or disable it.

5. Service Providers and Disclosure

We do not operate a market for personal information or sell it directly. We disclose data to Google, Apple, and advertising technology providers or vendors participating in Google's ad services, as needed for the services described in this policy. The advertising providers involved may vary by region, consent choice, and Google's configuration; where required, the UMP message presents the applicable vendors and purposes. Some privacy laws may define personalized advertising disclosures as “sharing”; the consent message and device controls described above provide the choices available for your region.

  • Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's ad technology providers — support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy — apple.com/legal/privacy

6. Retention and Security

Local save data remains on your device until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Analytics, advertising, consent, and transaction information may be retained by Google, Apple, or participating advertising technology providers according to their settings and retention policies. We use access controls and limit event fields to what is useful for operating and improving the Game, but no electronic processing method is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Oblok is a casual puzzle game intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly ask children for personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the issue.

8. International Processing

Google, Apple, and participating advertising technology providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy documentation describes the safeguards they apply to international data transfers.

9. Your Requests

You can erase local game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, disable notifications in device settings, and review advertising choices as described in section 3. For questions or privacy requests, contact us at destek@drksistem.com. Because the Game has no player account, please do not send sensitive identifiers unless support specifically requires them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, service providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the latest revision.
