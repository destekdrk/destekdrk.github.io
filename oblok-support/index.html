Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Drag the pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Completed rows and columns burst. Clearing a row and a column with the same move is a cross-blast: it awards double points and an extra tray of pieces.

What game modes are there?

Adventure Road is the main campaign: 250 levels across 25 cities, starting in Istanbul and ending with the finale in Zurich. Zen is an endless, high-score mode with no objectives. Boosters available in both: Undo, Bomb, Hammer, Dot Block, and Shuffle.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved locally on your device. Oblok has no accounts and no cloud save, so clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game resets your progress.

My booster pack purchase did not appear

Check your internet connection and restart the game; pending transactions are usually delivered on the next launch. If the pack is still missing, email us the store order number (from your Google Play or App Store receipt) and we will investigate.

How do I change the language?

Oblok follows your device language and supports 57 languages. If a translation is not available, the game falls back to English.

Are there ads?

Oblok is free to play. It shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitials between levels. Where required, a consent message lets you review your privacy options; purchases can supplement boosters. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a bug?

Email destek@drksistem.com with your device model, OS version, game version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.

About Oblok

Oblok is a jewel block puzzle built with Flutter for Android and iOS. It is offline-first: no account is required and your save data stays on your device. Current version: 1.0.0+1.
