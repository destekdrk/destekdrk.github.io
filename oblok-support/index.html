Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Drag the pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board, or tap a piece and then tap the board. Completed rows and columns burst. Clearing a row and a column with the same move is a cross-blast, which refills your tray with fresh pieces.

What game modes are there?

Adventure Path is the main campaign: 250 levels across 25 cities, starting in Istanbul and ending with the finale in Zürich. Every city closes with a boss level. Zen is an endless, high-score mode with no objectives and no fail state. The five boosters — Undo, Bomb, Hammer, Dot Block and Shuffle — work in both.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved locally on your device. Oblok has no accounts and no cloud save, so clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game resets your progress and it does not transfer to a new device.

Can I play offline?

Zen mode plays fully offline. The Adventure Path needs an internet connection, so new campaign levels cannot be started while you are offline. If you lose your connection during a level, the game waits and continues once it is back. Ad-funded rewards also need a connection.

My booster pack purchase did not appear

Check your internet connection and restart the game; pending transactions are usually delivered on the next launch. If the pack is still missing, email us the store order number (from your Google Play or App Store receipt) and we will investigate.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com for iOS, or Play Store > Order history for Android.

Are there ads?

Oblok is free to play. Rewarded ads are always optional and only start when you choose them. Interstitials appear only after a level you have won — never during play and never in levels 1–10 of the Adventure Path. Daily safety caps apply: 25 rewarded views and 8 interstitials per day. See the Privacy Policy for what the ad providers process.

The game stopped offering me ads

That is the daily cap, not a fault. When a cap is reached the offer button hides itself and returns the next day.

I watched an ad but didn't get the reward

A reward is granted when the ad network confirms the ad was watched to the end, so closing an ad early gives nothing. When no network ad is available, the game plays its own short promo video instead and grants the same reward once it is 90% watched. If a reward is still missing after a completed ad, email us with your device model and the level number.

How do I change my advertising / privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy in the game; the entry is always available. Declining personalisation does not remove ads — it only stops them being personalised, and rewards stay available. On iOS the system tracking prompt is shown only if you accepted personalisation.

I'm stuck on a level

Try the five boosters. When you run out of moves on a level you are losing, the game hands you one extra move to finish it. Every 25 stars also earns a spin on the wheel and a new block for your collection. If you would rather play without level goals, switch to Zen mode from the top bar.

How do I change the language?

Oblok follows your device language and supports 57 languages. If a translation is not available, the game falls back to English.

How do I report a bug?

Email destek@drksistem.com with your device model, OS version, game version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.
